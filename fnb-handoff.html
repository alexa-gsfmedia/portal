Client Background

What First National Bank is and why this video matters to them.

Business overview

  • • Community bank with 23 branches across Tennessee and Arkansas. The Paragould, AR campus at 200 W Court St is the downtown home of the bank.
  • • People stay. Several employees have worked there for 50-plus years, and some people in photos from 40 years ago still make loans today.
  • • The call center answers every call with a person, not AI. Customers love it.
  • • Eleanor and Faith run marketing for the whole bank with no marketing director. Their current agency is out of Jonesboro, AR, and its contract ends around October 2026.
  • • Make sure files and emails go to First National Bank (Arkansas and Tennessee). First National Bank of Tennessee in Livingston is a different company.

Why this project

  • • The Paragould branch finished a renovation, and employees are moving back in. The bank wants a video out before the holidays while the renovation is still news.
  • • The campaign theme already exists: "Our roots are in Paragould. Our roots run deep."
  • • Eleanor called this a risk because the bank has never worked with GSF. They need the project done, so the first execution matters.
  • • Eleanor's path: GSF delivers a great video, she takes it to Blake, then budget for a larger partnership gets approved.

Existing Marketing Efforts

What the bank has in place, and where it needs help.

Current state

  • • A Jonesboro agency handles creative production. The bank's own team runs most execution and ad placement.
  • • A third-party vendor runs display and geofencing ads. Do not position GSF against that vendor.
  • • Paid social runs in bursts around specific products. A deposit campaign ran about 45 days starting around June 22.
  • • Past video leaned toward financial literacy, internal training and product education. Little customer-facing video.

Pain points

  • • The current agency does not understand the bank's personality, and it stopped bringing ideas.
  • • The bank wants a polished brand that does not feel stuffy or generic.
  • • Social content leans on stock imagery. Real people and real places are the gap GSF can fill.
  • • Turnaround from the current agency was slow enough that Eleanor stopped trusting it on deadlines.

Client Preferences and Guidelines

What Eleanor asked for on the Sept 25 and Oct 7 calls.

Eleanor's direction

  • • A story piece, not only a renovation video. It covers the bank's history in downtown Paragould, then and now.
  • • Use old photos and footage, longtime employees, and the change from the past to today.
  • • Keep the theme "Our roots are in Paragould. Our roots run deep."
  • • Polished for a bank, but warm and personal. Nothing corporate or generic.
  • • GSF owns the creative: concept, shot list and story direction. Eleanor does not want to direct the agency.

Ideas to bring to the brainstorm

  • • Pair archival photos with the same spot today, and animate the old photos with AI.
  • • Feature employees with long tenure, including anyone who appears in an old photo and still works there.
  • • Show the call center and the people who answer every call.
  • • Show downtown Paragould, since the bank's identity is tied to the town.
